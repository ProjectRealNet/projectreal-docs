Giai ma dau vao Base64 bang Base64 decoder va tra ve cac byte da giai ma duoi dang Lua string.

## Cú pháp

```lua
base64_decode(data: string): string
```

## Alias

- `base64decode`
- `base64.decode`
- `crypt.base64.decode`
- `crypt.base64_decode`
- `crypt.base64decode`

## Đối số

| Name | Type | Required | Mô tả |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data` | `string` | Yes | Dau vao Base64 duoc truyen vao Base64 decoder. |

## Giá trị trả về

| Name | Type | Mô tả |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `decoded` | `string` | Cac byte da giai ma duoc tra ve duoi dang Lua string voi do dai ro rang. |

## Ví dụ

Giai ma du lieu Base64 va doc Lua string duoc tra ve.

```lua
local encoded = base64_encode("hello world")
local decoded = base64_decode(encoded)

print(decoded)
print(base64decode(encoded))
print(base64.decode(encoded))
```
